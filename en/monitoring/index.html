Food security monitoring

We track the availability and variety of staple foods and the cost of a food basket. The pilot covers Kyiv and Rivne.

What we measure

We track availability and prices across 18 product categories. We measure variety as the share of observations with at least three brands or types available.

We estimate the cost of a basket using fixed quantities of 17 items and compare its monthly cost with the statutory subsistence minimum for a working-age adult.
